N.S. & Associates offers consultancy services in water management systems: Rainwater Harvesting (RWH), storm water works, water management systems, and green building design and certification.

Since 2001 we have provided sustainable, eco-friendly solutions to India's growing water supply problem. Projects span residential buildings, IT complexes, commercial and industrial complexes over areas from 5 acres to 500 acres.

Beyond rainwater harvesting, we consult on green certifications such as USGBC LEED, IGBC Green Homes and GRIHA, design energy-efficient buildings and eco-housing projects, and provide clearances including High Rise and MoEF.

Our technical team consists of highly qualified engineers and geologists with vast field experience.

Mission

To increase awareness of the long-term benefits of rainwater harvesting among people and institutions through training workshops and demonstrations, and to take this technology pan India and to a global level.

Vision

To be known as India's top rainwater harvesting company by achieving water self-sufficiency for as many people as possible, bridging the gap between demand and supply.
