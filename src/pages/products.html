Vee-Wire vs the conventional pit

DetailConventional designApollo Vee-Wire design
Continuous slotsSlots are scatteredContinuous slot opening across the periphery and length of the screen
CloggingHighly susceptible as settling impurities and floating matter reduce media permeabilityInwardly widening V-slots leave no room for sand to stick, so efficiency holds for years
Open areaLess open areaMore than 2x the open area of slotted pipe, with lower frictional head loss
Sand filtrationPrecise slot selection is not possibleSlots down to 0.15 mm chosen to local sand, for longer borewell life
CleaningOnce the top layer chokes, percolation drops rapidlyWater enters horizontally from the pit bottom, so the top stays open and recharge continues
MonitoringPit filled with media cannot be inspectedOperating condition of the filter pit is easy to monitor

Three ways to harvest rain

  1. 01

    Storage in collection tanks

    Best where rain falls through the year or strata are impermeable. Rooftop water passes an Apollo online filter before storage.

  2. 02

    Recharge from rooftop runoff

    Roof water is piped to a filtration or settlement tank and into a recharge well. Preferred where rain is short and the water table is shallow.

  3. 03

    Recharge from ground runoff

    Open-area runoff is piped to a recharge dug well or borewell, with a Vee-Wire filter kit at the mouth so the aquifer is not contaminated.
