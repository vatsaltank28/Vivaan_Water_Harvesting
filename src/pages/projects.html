HPCL Residential Colony

Chembur, Mumbai

35,000+ L/dayBorewell recharging capacity

Before

  • 13 borewells, 6 of which went dry every April
  • Gardening totally dependent on borewell water
  • Water tankers required from April onwards
  • Heavy local extraction had lowered the groundwater table

After

  • Recharging capacity above 35,000 L/day
  • 50% of rooftop rainwater harvested by diverting front-side downtakes to the garden borewell
  • Borewell yield up from 10,000 to 20,000 L/day
  • Water level rose from 35 ft to 22 ft and quality improved

Ambrosial Developers, Easter Heights

4th Road, Santacruz, Mumbai

4 to 5 yearsImplementation cost recovered

Before

  • Borewell drilled in 2007, yield 15,000 L/day
  • Brackish aquifer encountered 50 ft below
  • 5,000 L/day needed for flushing and gardening
  • 182 tankers a year at Rs. 91,000 annually

After

  • Groundwater table increased
  • Non-potable water became usable for flushing and gardening
  • Implementation cost Rs. 3,50,000
  • Maintenance Rs. 6,000 a year, one cleaning before monsoon

Mahindra & Mahindra

Zahirabad

≈80,000 m³/yrRecharge from 45 proposed pits

Before

  • 400 m³/day needed for production
  • Demand met by tankers or borewell water

After

  • 500 m³ collection pond fulfils the water requirement
  • Runoff passes oil trap, sedimentation and filtration chambers, then flows by gravity to the pond
  • 45 recharging pits proposed, approx. 80,000 m³ recharged annually
  • Borewell water available throughout the year

Prince of Wales Museum (CSMVS)

Kala Ghoda, Mumbai. Heritage building near the coastline

35,000+ L/dayBorewell recharging capacity

Before

  • 10,000 L/day needed for gardening
  • Gardening totally dependent on borewell water
  • Coastal site with salinity risk

After

  • Recharging capacity above 35,000 L/day
  • 50% of rooftop rainwater diverted to the existing garden borewell
  • Borewell yield up from 10,000 to 20,000 L/day
  • Water level rose from 35 ft to 22 ft and quality improved
