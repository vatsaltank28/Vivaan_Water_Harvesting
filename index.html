System

Every roof is a catchment.

Rooftop and surface runoff is captured through downtake pipes and storm drains, then routed for storage or recharge.

  • Rooftop RWH: terrace water to holding tanks
  • Surface RWH: storm-drain runoff to recharge pits
  • Mandatory in Maharashtra for plots over 300 m²

Pre-filter

Clean water starts at the roof.

Product by Chaitanya Apollo Rooftop Box Filter Fits any downtake pipe. Filters down to 500 micron with a first-flush valve, no moving parts and almost zero water wastage.

Filter

Filtration that does not clog.

Product by Chaitanya Apollo Vee-Wire Filter Screen Kit Continuous V-shaped stainless steel slots, BIS 8110:2000. More than 2x the open area of slotted pipe, with zero maintenance cost.

Store

Store it for the dry months.

Product by Chaitanya Modular Water Storage Tanks ColorBond high-tensile steel shell with a potable-grade liner that resists algae, for large reservoir storage.
