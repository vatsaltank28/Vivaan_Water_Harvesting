N.S. & Associates in India’s Top 5 Vendor in the Rainwater Harvesting Market From 2017 to 2021: Technavio

N.S. & Associates in India’s Top 5 Vendor in the Rainwater Harvesting Market From 2017 to 2021: Technavio

LONDON-(BUSINESS WIRE)-Technavio has announced the top five Rainwater harvesting Consultants in India in their recent rainwater harvesting market in India 2017-2021 report. This market research report also lists 14 other prominent vendors that are expected to impact the market during the forecast period.

Competitive vendor landscape

According to the research analysis, the rainwater harvesting market in India is diversified because of the presence of a vast number of vendors. In developed and developing regions, the regional vendors dominate the market, thereby increasing competition. The market is expected to witness rapid growth in the future because of the growing scarcity of water. It is also expected that increased government support will boost the growth of the market significantly during the forecast period, which in turn will drive the competition in the rainwater harvesting market in India.

The top 5 major vendors in the Indian market according to the alphabetical order are as follows:

  • D&D Ecotech Services
  • KRG India
  • N.S. & Associates
  • Osmosis India
  • Water field technology

Top Rainwater Harvesting Consultants in india N.S. & Associates

N.S. & Associates provides sustainable and eco-friendly solutions to the prevailing problem of water supply in India. The company provides technically effective, innovative, customized and reasonably priced designs of rainwater harvesting system. The company has completed the designing of rainwater harvesting systems for more than 2,500 projects from both the public and private sector at multiple locations in India.

“Rooftop rainwater harvesting systems are now mandatory for new buildings in almost all of India’s 28 states and seven federally administered union territories. Government’s constant efforts to improve the residential infrastructure plays a vital role in supporting the growth of the rainwater harvesting market,” says Thanikachalam Chandrasekaran, a lead water and waste management research analyst from Technavio.
